## Space rail

- Clicking a space opens it in the sidebar, with filters for all chats, rooms and direct messages.
- **All chats** and **Outside spaces** open the same way. Outside spaces has a new icon and covers every chat that isn't in a space, including direct messages.
- The DMs filter under All chats replaces the old direct chats view.
- Unread rooms now appear below your spaces.
- The yellow marker shows which view you're in, including a filter inside a space.
- The rail is a bit bigger. Opening and closing a space is animated when **Ignore reduce motion** is on.

## Room list

- The open room has a stronger highlight.
- New **Room list color** setting for the accent colour when all rooms share one colour, which is now the default. Per-room colours are still available as a toggle.
- New header button to mark all rooms as read.

## Known issue

- Read receipts aren't always sent, so a room can stay unread after you've read it. Marking it read in another client clears it.
