## Colour that means something

- Sender colours are now handed out per room. The first time someone speaks in a room they get
  the palette colour farthest from everyone already talking there, and a custom colour you have
  set counts as taken, so two people in a small room no longer land on the same violet.
  Assignments are remembered, so nobody changes colour when someone new joins.
- Each run of a person's messages carries a thin rail in their colour beside the avatar, the
  avatar wears a ring in the same colour, and the name sits on a faint plate of it. Sender
  names are a step bigger and at full strength.
- Generated letter avatars and reply-quote spines use the same colour as the name they belong
  to. Reply spines previously ignored a custom colour; they no longer do.

## Room list

- A new **Unread** section at the top gathers every room with unread messages, above Rooms and
  Direct messages, including inside a space's Rooms or DMs view. The room you are reading stays
  put until you leave it. Toggle it under Appearance.
- Room names are set as small uppercase labels in one quiet ink grey on every row, with the
  open room's name in white. The glyph after the name carries the accent colour, and the
  preview line shows who spoke in their own colour.
- Uniform room colour now applies to every row, not just the resting ones, which was a bug.
  Resting rows are no longer dimmed; the open room's wash and the unread bars carry the emphasis.
- The unread bar and the open-room bar are the same height, and the band that opens under a
  parted space tile is a warmer, lighter shelf so it stands apart from the list.

## Reactions

- Clicking a reaction you have already added now removes it. Chips you have reacted with are
  marked, a chip dims while your change is on its way, and the count updates immediately
  instead of waiting for the server.

## Known issue

- Read receipts are not always sent, so a room can stay marked unread after you have read it.
  Marking it read from another client clears it. Still under investigation.
