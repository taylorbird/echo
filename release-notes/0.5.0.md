## Sender colours

- Sender colours are now picked per room, so people in the same room are less likely to share a colour. Custom colours you've set are respected, and people keep their colour when someone new joins.
- Each person's messages have a coloured line beside them, their avatar has a matching ring, and their name has a light tint behind it. Names are slightly bigger.
- Letter avatars and reply quotes use the sender's colour, including custom colours.

## Room list

- New **Unread** section at the top lists every room with unread messages, including inside a space. The room you're reading stays in place until you leave it. You can turn it off under Appearance.
- Room names are small grey capitals, with the open room in white. Room icons use the accent colour, and message previews show the sender in their colour.
- The shared room colour now applies to every room, not only inactive ones. Inactive rooms are no longer dimmed.
- The unread bar and the open-room bar are the same height. The area under an open space is lighter so it stands out from the list.

## Reactions

- Clicking a reaction you've already added removes it. Your reactions are highlighted, and counts update straight away.

## Known issue

- Read receipts aren't always sent, so a room can stay unread after you've read it. Marking it read in another client clears it.
