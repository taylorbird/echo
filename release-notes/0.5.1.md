## A quieter timeline

- The sender rail, the avatar ring and the plate behind the name are gone. After a week of
  use the three together made every room look busy, and they crowded the spine on quoted
  messages so a reply showed two lines instead of one.
- Sender names keep their colour at full strength and step up a size, to match the room list's
  room names, so the name still leads the row.
- The avatar gutter closes back up to where it was before 0.5.0.
- The spine beside a quoted or replied-to message is now one neutral grey in every room instead
  of the quoted person's colour. Their name and letter avatar inside the quote keep the colour.
- Room-aware sender colours, the Unread section and the reaction toggle from 0.5.0 are unchanged.

## Known issue

- Read receipts are not always sent, so a room can stay marked unread after you have read it.
  Marking it read from another client clears it. Still under investigation.
