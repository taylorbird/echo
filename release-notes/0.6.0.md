## Title bar

- The title bar is gone. The window buttons now sit in the space rail, and the panes reach the top of the window.
- The app name no longer appears inside the window.

## Font

- Everything uses Inter. Space Grotesk is gone.
- Room names in the sidebar are in normal case, a bit bigger, and white.

## Unread and mentions

- Unread rooms show a blue dot. Rooms where you were mentioned show a red **@** badge.
- These used to be the same red, with a pulse for mentions. With Reduce Motion on there was no way to tell them apart.

## Recent

- New **Recent** filter in the space rail, next to All chats, Rooms and Direct messages. It lists rooms and direct messages together, most recent first.

## Sign-in and first sync

- The sign-in and first sync screens have been redesigned.
- First sync explains what's happening, that it can take a few minutes, and that it only happens once. It counts rooms as they load.
- The room list shows placeholders while first sync runs.
- If sync fails, echo tells you whether it's retrying or has stopped. If it has stopped, it offers to restart.

## Room colours

- Room names are no longer coloured per room. They're white everywhere.
- The **Uniform room list color** setting has been removed.
- The accent colour still applies to room icons, and sender colours in the timeline haven't changed.

## Fixes

- Your own avatar now shows in the sidebar.
- Fixed a space showing as selected while its filters stayed closed.
- Fixed the description of the **Room list color** setting.

## Known issue

- Read receipts aren't always sent, so a room can stay unread after you've read it. Marking it read in another client clears it.
